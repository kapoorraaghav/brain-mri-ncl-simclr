# Publication notes

results and the current manuscript. Paper figures include illustrative MRI slices;
raw MRI datasets, patient-level activation tables, embeddings and new model weights
are excluded. Existing remote files are preserved without deletion or history rewrite.

resampling conditions on fitted models and analysis choices. Separate preserved
rerun weights do not recover the missing historical encoder states. Obtain inputs
under their applicable dataset terms. Dataset citations and attribution are in the
manuscript. No project software license has been selected.
